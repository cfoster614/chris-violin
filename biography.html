Biography

Christopher Stork is a violinist and violist based in Central New York. An active orchestral and chamber musician, he has performed throughout North America and Canada.

As a performer, Chris has worked with a variety of professional ensembles and musicians, with experience spanning orchestral repertoire, chamber music, and live events.

Alongside performing, Chris is an experienced teacher who has worked with students of varying ages and ability levels.

Whether performing on stage or working one-on-one with a student, Chris brings the same curiosity, attention to detail, and enthusiasm for music to his work.
