About Chris

Chris Stork is a violinist based in Baldwinsville, New York. He is a member of The Syracuse Orchestra and has performed throughout North America and Canada as a solist, chamber musician, and orchestral player.

Chris is dedicated to sharing his love of music through thoughtful, effective teaching that meets eac student where they are and helps them grow.
