Lessons

Whether you're an adult picking up the violin for the first time or a parent hoping to prepare your child for NYSSMA, my goal is to help my students grow as confident, expressive, and independent musicians.

Teaching Philosophy

Christopher Stork is a violinist and violist based in Central New York. An active orchestral and chamber musician, he has performed throughout North America and Canada.

As a performer, Chris has worked with a variety of professional ensembles and musicians, with experience spanning orchestral repertoire, chamber music, and live events.

Who I Teach

Beginners

Building a comfortable technical foundation and good practice habits in a supportive environment.

Intermediate/Advanced

Developing technique, repertoore, musical expression, and performance skills to reach your next level.

Adult Students

Whether dusting off the fiddle or picking one up for the first time, experienced and beginner adult students are welcome.
